What happens after you send the form

Your request lands with us the moment you press send. During business hours we call back within the hour to confirm details and book a window; after hours, scheduled requests are called first thing in the morning, and anything marked as an emergency is treated as a phone call — but please do call for those, because a ringing phone is faster than any form. On the day, your plumber calls when they're on the way, diagnoses on site, and writes an estimate before any work beyond the diagnosis starts.
