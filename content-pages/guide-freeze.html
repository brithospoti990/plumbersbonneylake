Why Lake Tapps freezes first

The lake isn't the Plateau's coldest spot, but its homes are the most exposed. Waterfront lots slope toward the water, which means crawl spaces with open vents facing the wind, supply lines that run the full length of the house to a lakeside kitchen or bath, and outdoor plumbing on the cold side: deck faucets, dock lines, boat-wash bibs. Many homes started as summer cabins and were winterized incrementally — a crawl space insulated on three sides, a line added for the hot tub that nobody wrapped. Add a water heater in an uninsulated garage and a wind off the water, and a 22°F night does damage that a 22°F night in Tehaleh wouldn't.

Room by room

Crawl space. The number one source of burst-pipe calls on the lake. Close the vents for winter (open them again in spring), insulate every supply line with foam sleeves, run heat tape on lines within a few feet of a vent, and make sure the vapor barrier is intact. If you've never been under your house, this is the year.

Garage. A water heater beside an uninsulated garage door loses a lot of heat on a cold night, and the pipes above it are the first to freeze. Insulate the lines, keep the door closed, and consider a draft baffle. A heater past its life is more likely to fail on the coldest morning; check the date on the rating plate before winter.

Outdoor faucets. Hoses off, standard bibs covered, frost-free bibs drained. Replace any that dripped this season. Our hose bib guide has the full walk-through.

Dock and waterfront lines. Blow them out and close the shut-off. A dock line without a drain-down valve should get one.

Irrigation. Blow out the zones and the backflow assembly; a frozen RPBA body cracks and that's a replacement.

Vacant or part-time homes. Thermostat at 55°F minimum, interior doors open, cabinet doors open on exterior walls. For long absences, a full drain-down is the safe option. A leak-detection shut-off valve that closes the main automatically is the best insurance a part-time lake home can buy.

What a burst costs — and why the plan is cheap

A half-inch supply line at Bonney Lake pressure releases several gallons a minute. Over a night in an empty crawl space that's hundreds of gallons into insulation, vapor barrier and subfloor, plus the drywall or flooring above it if the line is in a wall. The repair itself is often small; the restoration is not. Every item in the plan above costs less than the deductible on that claim, and most of them are a single Saturday. A leak-detection shut-off valve on the main, which closes automatically when flow doesn't stop, is the one upgrade we'd put ahead of all the others for a part-time lake home.

If a pipe freezes anyway

Open the faucet it serves so melting ice has somewhere to go. Warm the pipe gently from the faucet end toward the frozen section — a hair dryer, warm towels, a space heater in the room, never an open flame. Watch for leaks as it thaws; ice splits pipe, and the leak appears when flow returns. If you can't reach the section, if it's already leaking, or if the main itself froze, close the main and call (253) 465-7734. The water heater in a cold snap

A water heater that's marginal all year tends to quit on the coldest morning: inlet water in the low 40s°F means longer recovery, a tired element or a weak thermocouple finally gives up, and the family wakes to no hot water on a day the garage is at 30°F. If your heater is past ten years, has a dripping relief valve, or rumbles when it heats, replacing it before winter is cheaper than replacing it during one. We stock common sizes for same-day replacement, and we add the expansion tank and strapping that most lake-home installs are missing.

Docks, boats and the shoreline

Dock water lines are the most-forgotten plumbing on the lake. Many were added after the house was built, run above ground along the bank, and have no shut-off and no drain. Blown out and valved off, they survive winter; left charged, they split and leak unnoticed until the lake is in use again. While you're at it, a boat-wash bib or a dock hose station should be frost-free and on its own interior shut-off. We can add both in an afternoon.
