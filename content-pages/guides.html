Why we write these

Most plumbing advice online is written for everywhere, which means it's right for nowhere in particular. The questions we get on the Plateau have local answers: whether your water is hard depends on whether you're on Bonney Lake's springs or Tacoma's river water; whether your sewer line is at risk depends on the firs in your yard and the decade your house was built; whether you need a permit depends on which side of a city line you sit. These guides answer those questions for here, with the same honesty we bring to an estimate. Use the tools, and call (253) 465-7734 when you want a plumber to confirm what they told you.
