Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect what we need to schedule and perform plumbing work, we don't sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

Call (253) 465-7734Get a written estimate

Effective October 6, 2026. Plumbers Bonney Lake, 15121 198th Ave E, 3rd Floor, Ste Executive, Bonney Lake, WA 98391.

What we keep, at a glance

Information you give us

Name, phone, email, service address, and the description of the job you send through our form, tell us on the phone, or provide at a visit. We use it to schedule, perform, invoice and warranty the work, and to follow up about that work.

Information collected automatically

Standard web-server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring site) and basic analytics used to understand which pages help people. We don't run advertising trackers on this site.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We don't share it with third parties except service providers who help us run the business (hosting, form storage, scheduling, payment processing, a licensed electrician or septic contractor on your job) and only for that purpose; or when the law requires it.

Your choices

Email or call us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to opt out of follow-up messages. Deleting records of permitted work may be limited by record-keeping rules.

Details

Forms and phone calls. When you submit the service request form, the information is stored securely by our hosting and form providers so our office can call you back. Phone calls may be logged with your number and the reason for the call. Emergency calls are prioritized based on what you tell us.

Cookies. This site uses only the cookies and local storage needed for it to function (for example, remembering a form draft or a tool's inputs on your device). No advertising cookies are set by us.

Embedded maps. Pages include Google Maps embeds, which load from Google and are governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children. This site is for adults arranging plumbing services; we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, and we limit access to people who need it to do your job.

Changes. We'll post updates to this policy here with a new effective date.

Contact. Questions about privacy: call (253) 465-7734 or write to the address above.
