What customers tend to mention

We don't quote reviews here, but the themes are consistent and we work to keep them that way: the phone gets answered by a person; the plumber arrives when we said; the estimate is written and the price doesn't change; the problem gets explained in plain language; and the house is left clean. When a review is critical, Ashino reads it the same day and calls the customer. That's the whole policy.

Verify us before you book

A review is one signal. Two others matter more: our Washington plumbing contractor registration and our plumbers' L&I certifications, both printed in the footer of every page and checkable on the Department of Labor & Industries contractor lookup. We're also happy to give references for larger jobs — repipes, sewer replacements, remodels — from customers in your area.

Frequently asked questions

Why aren't the reviews shown on this page?

Because reviews copied onto a company's own site can be edited or cherry-picked. We link to the platforms where reviews are verified and public, so you can read every one of them — including any critical ones — exactly as written.

Where should I leave a review?

Google is the most useful for neighbors searching for a plumber; Yelp and Nextdoor help too. The buttons on this page take you straight to the right form.

What if I had a problem with a job?

Call Ashino directly at (253) 465-7734 before anything else. We'd rather fix it than read about it, and we stand behind our work.
