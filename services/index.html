Plumbing Services in Bonney Lake, WA

Twenty-two plumbing services, one local crew. Everything below is done by Washington-certified plumbers based on 198th Ave E in Bonney Lake, with a written estimate before work starts and the permits the City or Pierce County require.

Emergency & water treatment

When something is actively leaking, backing up or frozen, our emergency plumber dispatches 24 hours a day from inside Bonney Lake. For the slower problems — scale, taste, sediment and iron — we test your water before recommending a water softener or a filtration system, because City spring water, Tacoma Water and private wells on the Plateau all need different treatment.

Water heaters

Tank and tankless, gas and electric, repair and replacement. We stock the common sizes for same-day water heater replacement, install tankless units sized for Western Washington's cold winter inlet water, and solve the two problems that shorten water heater life on the Plateau: thermal expansion in closed systems (expansion tanks) and the long wait for hot water at the far end of the house (recirculation). Hose bibs live here too, because a frozen one is the other classic winter call.

Kitchen & appliance plumbing

Garbage disposals, dishwashers, ice maker lines and instant hot water dispensers: the small jobs that are easy to get wrong and expensive when they leak inside a cabinet. We install with proper air gaps, shut-offs and drain connections.

Bathroom & fixtures

From a running toilet or dripping faucet to a complete tub or shower replacement or a walk-in bathtub for safer bathing, plus general fixture installation for remodels and upgrades.

Pipes, drains & remodels

Drain cleaning for the clog in front of you, and drain and sewer line repair when the camera shows roots or a broken joint. Water line repair for leaks between the meter and the house. Backflow preventer installation and testing to meet the City of Bonney Lake's cross-connection program. And repipes and remodel plumbing for homes with failing galvanized or polybutylene pipe, or a kitchen and bath that's moving walls.

How every job works

You call and a person answers. We tell you an honest arrival window. On site, your plumber diagnoses the cause, writes an estimate with options, and nothing beyond the diagnosis is billed until you approve it. Trucks are stocked for most repairs on the first visit. Permits are pulled when required and inspections scheduled. Work is warrantied, and we note anything we saw that you'll want to deal with later — without pressure to do it today. We serve Bonney Lake, Lake Tapps, Sumner, Buckley, Tehaleh, Orting, South Prairie, Puyallup, Enumclaw, Auburn, Edgewood and Prairie Ridge; see all service areas.
