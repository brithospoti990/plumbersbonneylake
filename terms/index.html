The essentials

Estimates and approval

After diagnosis we provide a written estimate. Work beyond the diagnosis begins only with your approval. If conditions found during the work change the scope, we stop and get your approval for the change before continuing.

Payment

Payment is due on completion unless the estimate says otherwise. Larger projects may require a deposit and progress payments stated in the estimate. Diagnostic and emergency arrival fees disclosed on the phone apply whether or not you proceed with further work.

Warranty

Our workmanship is warrantied for the period stated on your invoice. Manufacturer warranties on parts and fixtures are passed through to you. Warranty excludes damage from freezing, misuse, lack of required maintenance, work by others, pre-existing conditions we identified in writing, and fixtures supplied by the customer (installation workmanship is still covered).

Access, permits and inspections

You agree to provide safe access to the work area and to the water, gas and electrical shut-offs. Where permits are required we obtain them in our name and schedule inspections; permit fees are listed on the estimate. Work requiring other trades (electrical, drywall, septic) is coordinated and billed as stated in the estimate.

Limits

We're not responsible for pre-existing damage, hidden conditions we could not reasonably detect, or consequential losses beyond the cost of the work, to the extent permitted by Washington law. Nothing here limits rights you have under Washington's consumer protection laws.

Using this website

The content, tools and calculators on plumbersbonneylake.com are general information for homeowners in our service area. They are not a substitute for an on-site diagnosis by a licensed plumber, and results from the interactive tools are estimates. We may change the site at any time. You may not scrape, republish or misrepresent the site's content.

Cancellation

Scheduled appointments can be cancelled or moved by phone without charge with reasonable notice. Emergency dispatches already en route may incur the disclosed arrival fee.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Washington. Any dispute will be resolved in the courts of Pierce County, Washington, after a good-faith attempt to resolve it directly with us — call Ashino first at (253) 465-7734.
